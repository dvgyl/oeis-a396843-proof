## Reference proof

[A396093](https://oeis.org/A396093) is excluded from the active contribution set. The [agentic-conjectures proof](https://github.com/umaia1234/agentic-conjectures/blob/c66cca68e85af87d46a7f3431b2602f3c9ebfcf0/problems/oeis-a396093/README.md) is present in a commit dated 11 August 2026. It proves the same complete parity classification. The present [paper](preprints/A396093-Odd-coefficients-of-the-third-iterate/paper.pdf) and [Lean formalization](lean/Entries/A396093.lean) remain as reference material. The present formalization starts from composition. The earlier formalization starts from the recorded recurrence. This difference does not make the mathematical theorem new.

The reference theorem remains in the full library checks. Thus the counts of 28 papers, 240 entry theorems, and 56 selected Comparator statements include reference material. They are not counts of new results.

## Existing corrections and results

The earlier result for [A397588](https://oeis.org/A397588) is not in this collection. Existing theta identities, Catalan congruences, Frobenius identities, and source formulas are used with attribution. No priority claim follows from the absence of an earlier proof in a search.
