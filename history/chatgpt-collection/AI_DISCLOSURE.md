assistance; independent human review is still pending.

## Expanded version 2: attribution and limits

The newly added proofs address seven statements marked conjectural in the retrieved entries A396093, A389474, A389475, and A396102. Two are direct instances of classical Frobenius, which is not claimed as new mathematics. The implicit integer series are constructed, and their original functional equations and uniqueness are proved before the sequence-specific congruences are derived.

The seven Stirling periodicity applications are **formalizations of known results**, not newly solved open problems. Payam Danesh's 26 August 2026 preprint, *Eventual periodicity of weighted Stirling transforms and two conjectures of Bala*, Theorem 4.1 and Corollary 4.2, already proves the general integral-substitution theorem with a stronger Carmichael period: <https://doi.org/10.33774/coe-2026-4d6jr>. Schreyer's earlier Fubini periodicity work is also credited. The continuing presence of a conjecture label in an OEIS entry does not establish that its statement is still open.

The Stirling-number recurrence and part of the power-expansion Lean proof were adapted from an upstream Mathlib module. They are not wholly original AI-written code. The source file preserves its copyright/authors/license header; `third_party/mathlib/NOTICE.md` identifies the exact revision and adapted portions, and `Apache-2.0.txt` contains the license. The modular transfer and named specialization proofs were generated in this session.

The all-index finite Stirling-transform theorem is formalized. The formal-exponential generating-function identity identifying it with `G(exp(x)-1)` is explained in the manuscript but is not separately claimed to be a Lean theorem. The source-formula specializations and index ranges are recorded in `docs/ROUND2_COVERAGE.md`.

Endpoint counts include foundational and supporting results and must not be described as counts of newly solved conjectures.
