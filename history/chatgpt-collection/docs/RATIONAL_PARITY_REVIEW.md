## Exact source claim

The current [OEIS A396093 entry](https://oeis.org/A396093) defines the generating
function as the third compositional iterate of `B(x)=x/(1-x)^2`, with offset zero
and `a(0)=0`. It lists two parity conjectures: all positive even-indexed terms are
even, and `a(2n-1)` is even precisely when `n=5k-2` for a positive integer `k`.
The source was read on 24 October 2026. Targeted searches did not identify a
separate published proof; the search does not establish global priority.

The formal endpoints cover both conjectures and prove the stronger complete
description

\[
 a(n)\equiv
 \begin{cases}
  1\pmod2,& n\equiv1,3,7,9\pmod {10},\\
  0\pmod2,& n\equiv0,2,4,5,6,8\pmod {10}.
 \end{cases}
\]

## Definition audit

`RationalParity.B` is `X * geom 1 ^ 2`. The previously checked geometric series
has coefficient one at every index and satisfies `(1-X)*geom 1=1`. Consequently
this definition is exactly the formal series `x/(1-x)^2` over the integers.

`RationalParity.A` is `iter B 3`. Inspection of `Consecutive.Basic` confirms that
`iter B 0=X` and `iter B (n+1)=compose B (iter B n)`. Inspection of
`Hanna.Series.Basic` confirms that `compose` is actual Mathlib formal power-series
substitution, with the outer series as its first argument. The file also proves
`A_definition`, displaying the three copies of `B` explicitly. Thus no iteration
convention or shift is left implicit.

`RationalParity.a n` is the integer coefficient of this series. Its zero constant
coefficient matches the source's offset-zero data. The use of integer coefficients
is sufficient for the parity claims; no auxiliary positivity assertion is needed.

## Proof audit

Reduction of the integer series modulo two is proved to commute with the defining
composition. The characteristic-two calculation therefore applies to the actual
integer sequence, rather than to an independently introduced recurrence.

The successive denominator identities are exact identities of formal power series.
They yield

\[
 A(x)(1+x^{10})=x+x^3+x^7+x^9
 \qquad\text{in }\mathbf F_2[[x]].
\]

The comparison series is explicitly defined by the four residue classes above.
Its denominator identity is proved for every coefficient index. The small-index
case split covers only the start of a universally quantified coefficient proof;
the remaining indices use reduction by ten. It is not an assumption based on
observing finitely many sequence terms.

Cancellation of `1+X^10` is justified by a displayed formal inverse, obtained from
the geometric series. It does not assume that an arbitrary nonzero series is
invertible or that a ring with zero divisors is a field. Coefficient extraction
then proves `a396093_residue` for every natural index.

The final conversion uses the actual integer parity predicates. The theorem
`a396093_even_indices` includes index zero as an additional valid case.
`a396093_odd_indices_original` retains both positivity conditions from the source
and proves the exact existential expression `n=5*k-2`. The hypothesis `n>=1`
prevents truncated natural subtraction at `2*n-1` from altering the intended claim.
