# A195194: Parity of an infinitely nested series reversion

Accompanying paper: [Parity of an infinitely nested series reversion](../../preprints/A195194-Parity-of-an-infinitely-nested-series-reversion/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A195194.lean`](../Entries/A195194.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A195194`. There, for a sequence of weights `w`, the series $`F_j`$ of the solution is `tail w j`, and the series $`F^{[d]}_j`$ of the tower of height $`d`$ is `approximation w d j`. The proofs are in the module `lean/Families/NestedReversion.lean`.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
