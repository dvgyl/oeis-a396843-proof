# A355872: Residues modulo $`16`$ for a series defined by a bilateral theta-type equation

Accompanying paper: [Residues modulo $`16`$ for a series defined by a bilateral theta-type equation](../../preprints/A355872-Residues-modulo-16-for-a-bilateral-theta-type-equation/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A355872.lean`](../Entries/A355872.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A355872`. The construction over an arbitrary commutative ring, its compatibility with reduction of coefficients, and the congruence for the whole series modulo $`16`$ are in `lean/Families/BilateralTheta.lean` (namespace `Families.BilateralTheta`). The theorems in the table assume only equation (1), as stated in the entry; no congruence is assumed.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
