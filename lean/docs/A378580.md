# A378580: Remainders modulo $`4`$ for the series with $`A\bigl(x/A(x)\bigr)=\theta_3(x)`$

Accompanying paper: [Remainders modulo $`4`$ for the series with $`A\bigl(x/A(x)\bigr)=\theta_3(x)`$](../../preprints/A378580-Theta-quotient-substitution-modulo-4/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A378580.lean`](../Entries/A378580.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A378580`. In the Lean code, $`\theta`$ is called `theta`. The prefix `minus` in the names of the declarations refers to the quotient equation $`A(x/A)=\theta`$; the corresponding declarations for A378581 carry the prefix `plus`. The quotient $`x/A`$ is written `X * invOfUnit A 1`, the product of $`x`$ with the multiplicative inverse of $`A`$. The general constructions, for an arbitrary series $`T`$ with constant term $`1`$ over any commutative ring, are in `lean/Families/ThetaSubstitution.lean`. The Lean proof of Lemma 4.1 takes a slightly different route from the one above: it treats the product form through the equivalent equation $`G(G(x))=G(x)\,T(x)`$ for $`G=xU`$, and reduces the quotient form to the product form using $`U=1/V`$.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
