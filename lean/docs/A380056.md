# A380056: The coefficients of $`(e^x-1)/\cos 2x`$ modulo 4 and 5

Accompanying paper: [The coefficients of $`(e^x-1)/\cos 2x`$ modulo 4 and 5](../../preprints/A380056-Exponential-over-cosine-modulo-4-and-5/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A380056.lean`](../Entries/A380056.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A380056`. There the series $`e^x`$ and $`\cos x`$ are Mathlib's formal exponential and cosine series over $`\mathbb{Q}`$, with $`x`$ replaced by $`2x`$ in the cosine. The integer sequence is defined by the recurrence of Lemma 4.1, and the proofs are in the file `lean/Families/ExpCosine.lean`.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
