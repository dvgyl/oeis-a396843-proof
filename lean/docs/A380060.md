# A380060: Square indices and remainders modulo 4 in a theta-type series

Accompanying paper: [Square indices and remainders modulo 4 in a theta-type series](../../preprints/A380060-Square-indices-in-a-theta-type-series/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A380060.lean`](../Entries/A380060.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A380060`. There the one-sided sum $`\Sigma(A)`$ is written out coefficient by coefficient (only the indices $`j\le n`$ can contribute to $`x^n`$), and the multiplicative inverse of a series with constant term $`1`$ is written `unitInv`. The folding of the two-sided sum (Lemma 2.1) is proved in this paper but not in Lean. The proofs are in the file `lean/Families/ThetaRational.lean`.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
