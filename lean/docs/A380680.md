# A380680: Twice the squares, not the squares: remainders modulo 4 in a theta-type series

Accompanying paper: [Twice the squares, not the squares: remainders modulo 4 in a theta-type series](../../preprints/A380680-Twice-the-squares-not-the-squares/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A380680.lean`](../Entries/A380680.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A380680`. There the one-sided sum $`\Sigma(2)`$ is written out coefficient by coefficient (only the indices $`j\le n`$ can contribute to $`x^n`$), and the multiplicative inverse of a series with constant term $`1`$ is written `unitInv`. The folding of the two-sided sum (Lemma 2.1) is proved in this paper but not in Lean. The proofs are in the file `lean/Families/ThetaRational.lean`. Part (b) of Corollary 3.2 is a direct consequence of the theorem and is not stated separately in Lean.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
