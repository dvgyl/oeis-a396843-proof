## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A389536.lean`](../Entries/A389536.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A389536`. There the Catalan series appears as `scaledSolution 0`, the solution of a more general equation which, for the parameter value $`0`$, says $`C(x-x^2)=x`$; the series with this property is the same as the solution of $`C=x+C^2`$. The proofs are in the module `lean/Families/MahlerParity.lean`.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
