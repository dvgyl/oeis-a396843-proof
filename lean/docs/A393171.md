# A393171: Remainders modulo $`8`$ of a series built from the Catalan numbers

Accompanying paper: [Remainders modulo $`8`$ of a series built from the Catalan numbers](../../preprints/A393171-Remainders-modulo-8-from-the-Catalan-numbers/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A393171.lean`](../Entries/A393171.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A393171`. There the Catalan series is called `catalan`, and $`B`$ is defined as $`x+A^2`$; the theorems show that this series satisfies the equation of the entry and is its only normalized solution. The main proofs are in the module `lean/Families/CatalanComposition.lean`.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
