## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A396102.lean`](../Entries/A396102.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A396102`. In Lean, `family_source_existence r` and `family_source_equation r` state the case $`r+1`$ of (1), so that $`r=0`$ in Lean corresponds to $`r=1`$ in this paper; the other declarations use the same $`r`$ as the paper. The proofs are in the file `lean/Families/InverseIterate.lean`. The consequences for OEIS A140094 and A067145 in Section 6 are not part of the Lean formalization.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
