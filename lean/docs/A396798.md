# A396798: The iterates of a self-referential power series modulo $`8`$

Accompanying paper: [The iterates of a self-referential power series modulo $`8`$](../../preprints/A396798-Iterates-of-a-self-referential-series-modulo-8/paper.pdf)

## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A396798.lean`](../Entries/A396798.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A396798`. The general results for the whole family are proved in the folder `lean/Consecutive` of the repository; the last rows of the table give their full names. In Lean, iterates with negative index are built from the compositional inverse, exactly as in Section 2.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
