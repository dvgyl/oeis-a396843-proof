## Scope

The statements are in [`Entries/A396843.lean`](../Entries/A396843.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A396843`. There the left side of (3) is `Series.Psi m A`, which stands for `compose A (X*A - C m * X * A^2)`; the coefficients $`a_n(m)`$ and $`b_n(m)`$ are `a m n` and `b m n`. Sixteen principal statements about this family are collected in `lean/Hanna/Statements.lean`.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
