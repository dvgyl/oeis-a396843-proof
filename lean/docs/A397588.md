The statements are in [`Entries/A397588.lean`](../Entries/A397588.lean), in the namespace `OEIS.A397588`. There the derivative $`U'`$ of an integer series $`U`$ is Mathlib's formal derivative of power series, written `derivative` applied to $`U`$; it is defined coefficientwise by the formula of Section 2. The proofs are in the module `lean/Families/DifferentialParity.lean`.

Every declaration below depends only on Lean's standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The observations in the paper's Discussion section are not formalized.
